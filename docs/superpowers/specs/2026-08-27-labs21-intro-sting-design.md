Plays once per browsing session.\n\n---\n\n## Sting Video\n\nEncode a ~500ms mp4 from every 6th frame of the existing hero video:\n\n```bash\nffmpeg -i AI/video/labs21-hero.mp4 \\\n  -vf \"select='not(mod(n,6))',setpts=N/FRAME_RATE/TB\" \\\n  -r 96 -c:v libx264 -preset fast -crf 18 \\\n  -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart \\\n  AI/video/labs21-sting.mp4\n```\n\n- 48 frames (every 6th of 288), played at 96fps = 500ms\n- CRF 18 for hero-quality appearance\n- Output: ~200–500KB\n\n---\n\n## Behavior\n\n1. **Page loads** → a fullscreen `#intro-overlay` (black `#000` background) covers the entire viewport, `z-index: 9999`\n2. **Sting video** autoplays (muted) inside the overlay, `object-fit: cover`, centered\n3. **Video ends** (~500ms) → overlay fades out over 300ms (`opacity 1 → 0`, `transition: opacity 0.3s var(--ease-smooth)`)\n4. **Overlay removed** from DOM → landing page visible beneath\n5. **Session flag**: `sessionStorage.setItem('labs21_intro_seen', '1')` after first playback.
